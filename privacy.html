Privacy Policy

Glow – Monitor Brightness · Effective 15 November 2026

Glow doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It talks only to your own monitor, on your own network.

What Glow keeps on your Mac

  • The monitor you chose: its name, model, IP address and network (MAC) address, so Glow can find and wake it.
  • The pairing key the monitor gives Glow when you choose Allow, kept in your Mac's Keychain.

These never leave your Mac, except that the pairing key is sent back to the monitor itself with each command.

What Glow sends over the network

  • Requests to discover Samsung displays on your local network.
  • Commands to your monitor, such as "set brightness to 60%".
  • A "wake up" signal on your local network when you ask Glow to turn the monitor on.

All of this stays inside your home network. Glow has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code, and it never contacts anything on the internet.

Removing your data

Delete Glow from your Applications folder. To remove the pairing key as well, open Keychain Access and delete the item whose name starts with "com.subxan.glow".

Children

Glow collects no data from anyone, including children.
